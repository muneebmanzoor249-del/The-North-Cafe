Our Story

A cafe built for the north of the valley.

Tucked along the highway near Skuast, Wadura, The North Cafe was built on a simple idea: fast food doesn't have to mean forgettable food. Every burger is pressed to order, every shawarma rolled fresh, every plate sent out the way we'd want it ourselves.

From students and staff at the campus next door to families passing through Sopore, the counter stays busy — and the kitchen doesn't cut corners because of it.
